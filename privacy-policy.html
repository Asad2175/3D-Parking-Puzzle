Privacy Policy

3D Parking Puzzle: Escape Jam · Effective date: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the 3D Parking Puzzle: Escape Jam mobile application ("the App") handles information. The short version: the App itself collects no personal data and requires no account. The only data processing happens inside the third-party advertising SDK described in section 3.

1. Data the App Collects

None directly. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of its own, and it does not access your location, contacts, photos, files or any other personal information.

2. Game Progress

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, unlocked cars, daily-challenge streaks, and settings such as theme, language and sound) is stored only on your device, using local storage. It never leaves your device, and we have no access to it. Uninstalling the App deletes this data permanently.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (on devices with Google Play services) or Huawei Petal Ads (on Huawei devices). To serve and measure ads, these SDKs may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID of your device
  • IP address (which implies an approximate, city-level location)
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and tapped
  • Basic device information, such as model and OS version, for compatibility and fraud prevention

This data is collected and processed by the ad networks under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses ad data
  • Huawei Petal Ads: Petal Ads Privacy Statement

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's system settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Internet Use

The game itself works fully offline — every level, hint and feature functions without a connection. The network is used only to load ads when a connection is available.

5. Device Permissions

The App may use your device's vibration capability to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. This can be turned off in the App's Settings and involves no data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The App itself collects no personal data at all; ad serving is handled by Google AdMob under Google's policies, including its protections around child-directed treatment.

7. Data Deletion

All App data lives on your device: use "Reset Progress" in Settings to clear game data, or uninstall the App to delete everything. For data processed by the ad networks, see the links in section 3.

8. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices change (for example, if a future update adds online features), this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised before those changes take effect.
